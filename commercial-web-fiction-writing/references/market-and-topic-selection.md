# Market and Topic Selection

## Market Map

Common Chinese female-oriented web-fiction classes include modern romance, historical romance, fantasy romance, gaming/competitive fiction, derivative fiction, and realism. Each can split or combine by setting, plot engine, character archetype, tone, and special element.

Evaluate a proposed topic as:

- **Traditional:** durable audience; mature conventions mean success depends on plot, character appeal, prose, and atmosphere unless differentiated.
- **Hot:** easier audience recognition and faster initial traction, but severe substitution and competition; require a memorable distinction.
- **Distinctive:** smaller but often stickier audience, milder competition, and more room for novelty.
- **Sensitive/high-risk:** screen against platform rules, public order, age suitability, violence, sexual content, politics, crime glorification, and harmful value guidance.

## Selection Decision

1. Start with what the author genuinely likes and reads deeply; familiarity usually provides better convention knowledge and reader empathy.
2. If no strength is clear, choose the familiar option with the lowest execution burden.
3. Do not enter an unfamiliar trend based only on heat.
4. A robust beginner default is `traditional category + micro-innovation`.
5. Platform contests can reduce acquisition friction when their support and requirements fit the project.
6. “Narrow road” experimentation is more suitable after the author has mastered mainstream craft and completed enough work to understand market feedback.

## Platform Research

Research the intended submission platform, not an abstract market:

- Hot list: established demand and mature execution.
- New-book list: current packaging, openings, and emerging elements.
- Category ranking: category-specific pacing and reader contract.

Read representative books through their endings. Extract premise, core promise, stage goals, escalation pattern, payoff schedule, character arcs, and ending. Looking only at openings creates authors who can start strongly but cannot sustain a book.

## Platform Content Balance

For a free-reading platform, combine:

- accessibility, quick pacing, low entry cost, clear pleasure/pain, and strong emotion;
- story depth, coherent theme, stable world architecture, and a durable core narrative.

Reader sophistication changes over time. Refresh research instead of treating a formerly hot element as permanent.

## Micro-Innovation Axes

Innovate one or more controlled dimensions without obscuring the reader contract:

- character identity or relationship;
- category fusion or a current element;
- reversal of a known convention;
- central speculative device;
- timeline structure, such as countdown, displaced rebirth, or body/role exchange;
- escalation of a familiar payoff, such as personal regret becoming family-wide regret.

Innovation is valuable only when it creates a clearer, fresher core experience that the target reader can recognize.
