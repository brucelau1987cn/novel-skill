# Prose Voice and Final Quality Gate

## Default Prose Contract

Apply these rules to Chinese fiction drafting, rewriting, and polishing unless the user explicitly requests another style.

### Prohibited AI-shaped language

Do not use stock transitions or summary fillers such as:

- 总而言之
- 不仅如此
- 可以说
- 值得一提的是
- 首先 / 其次 / 最后 as a mechanical exposition frame

Also remove equivalent empty bridges, end-of-paragraph summaries, canned contrasts, and sentences that merely restate what the scene has already shown.

Do not replace one banned phrase with another template. Rewrite the causal or emotional movement directly.

### Rhythm

- Avoid evenly sized paragraphs and textbook-symmetrical prose.
- Vary paragraph length according to pressure, attention, and emotional impact.
- A single-sentence paragraph is permitted for a turn, pause, blow, realization, or hook. Do not repeat it at a predictable interval.
- Break clean rhetorical triplets and polished parallel lists when they sound generated rather than character-bound.
- Preserve readability: unpredictability does not mean obscurity.

### Show, Don't Tell

Do not announce abstract emotions such as “极度害怕”“非常美丽”“无比愤怒.” Render emotion through selected evidence:

- physical action and interruption
- involuntary bodily response
- handling of objects
- changes in voice, breathing, posture, distance, or gaze
- a pressure-tested choice
- one concrete environmental detail filtered through the viewpoint character

Use the smallest number of details that carry the strongest emotional weight. Do not mechanically add all five senses.

### Lexical force

- Prefer exact, active verbs over adjective and adverb stacks.
- Reject stale collocations when the viewpoint permits a sharper, more concrete expression.
- Figurative language must arise from the character's experience, profession, class, region, or current fixation.
- Do not chase novelty at the cost of clarity, continuity, or character voice.

### Subjective viewpoint

The narration must be filtered through a person or a clearly positioned narrator. Selection of detail, metaphor, judgment, and omission should reveal attitude. Avoid sterile omniscient neutrality unless the requested form truly requires it.

Characters may misunderstand. The narration must still preserve the difference between character belief and story fact.

## Continuity and Completeness Gate

Before delivery, reread the produced text rather than trusting the outline.

Check:

- Time: order, duration, daylight, deadlines, travel time.
- Space: entrances, exits, positions, objects, injuries, clothing, weather.
- Knowledge: who knows each fact and when they learned it.
- Character state: goal, mood, injury, resources, relationships, promises.
- Causality: each major action has a motive; each turn follows an event or choice.
- Voice: diction and behavior remain consistent with the viewpoint and character card.
- Promise: the scene delivers the expected emotional or plot value.
- Closure: the current action reaches an intentional stopping point; hooks do not substitute for missing causality.
- Hooks and foreshadowing: no accidental orphaned setup; deliberate long-term hooks remain tracked.

## Final De-AI Pass

Run a final prose pass after story revisions, because structural edits can reintroduce templates.

1. Search for the explicit prohibited phrases.
2. Mark repetitive sentence openings, symmetrical paragraphs, repeated contrast patterns, and repeated chapter-ending formulas.
3. Replace emotional labels with observable evidence where the scene needs embodiment.
4. Remove explanations that follow already legible action.
5. Read dialogue aloud mentally; ensure it is speakable and character-specific.
6. Inspect every paragraph ending for unnecessary summary.
7. Recheck continuity after every rewrite.

A clean keyword scan is necessary but not sufficient. If the cadence still feels uniform, over-explained, neutral, or frictionlessly polished, rewrite the passage.
