# IAA and Wireless-Flow Chapter Design

Use for IAA/free-ad-supported and wireless-flow commercial web fiction when no newer platform-specific evidence overrides it.

## Default Length

- Target: 2,000-2,500 Chinese characters/words as defined by the publishing workflow.
- Short warning: below 1,500.
- Long warning: above 3,000.

These are working ranges, not permission to pad or cut a live scene in half. Scene integrity, platform rules, and actual reader data override the default. Confirm how the target platform counts “字数.”

Do not state unsupported algorithm effects as facts. Treat claims about recommendation weights, ad placement, retention thresholds, and revenue as hypotheses until verified against current official rules or real dashboard data.

## A 2,000-Character Working Shape

Use as a pacing map, not a rigid mold:

- Opening 300-500: connect directly to the previous unresolved action or enter the current disturbance quickly. Reorientation should be brief and embedded in action.
- Development 800-1,000: tighten emotional pull, opposition, misunderstanding, bargaining, threat, or task difficulty. Every exchange changes pressure or knowledge.
- Turn/payoff about 500: release a small climax, counterattack, revelation, relationship shift, or reversal. The chapter must pay something, not only promise.
- Final about 200: after the local payoff, introduce a concrete new variable: threat, information, person, cost, abnormal event, deadline, or changed relationship.

Actual allocation may shift. A relationship chapter may spend more space on calibrated emotional movement; an action chapter may compress setup and expand the turn.

## Chapter-End Standard

A useful ending does both:

1. Completes or materially advances the chapter's current action.
2. Opens a specific next expectation.

Do not end after every issue is emotionally and causally sealed. Do not manufacture a fake cliffhanger by withholding information the viewpoint character would naturally perceive. Do not raise a question that the next chapter ignores.

Rotate hook mechanisms: consequence, reversal, information gap, new threat, relationship change, deadline, discovery, or an irreversible choice. Do not repeat the same sentence pattern.

## Chapter Review

- Is the previous chapter's hook addressed promptly?
- What changed in goal, relationship, information, danger, or status?
- Where is the chapter's earned payoff?
- Does the ending launch a real next action?
- Is any paragraph present only to reach the target length?
- Would splitting or combining chapters improve the emotional unit?
- Does the chapter preserve the prose contract and final quality gate?
