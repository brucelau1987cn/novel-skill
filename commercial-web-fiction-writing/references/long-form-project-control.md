# Long-Form Fiction Project Control

This reference adapts the useful engineering practices from `LAY-lgtm/novel-writing-framework` while rejecting unsupported universal thresholds.

## Rule Layers

Organize constraints by specificity:

1. Core story and prose principles.
2. Current platform rules and reader contract.
3. Genre/type rules.
4. This novel's story bible and deliberate style.
5. Current scene purpose.

When rules conflict, the more specific verified rule normally wins. Safety, originality, continuity, and explicit user instructions remain non-negotiable.

## Recommended Project Files

- `故事圣经.md`: canonical world rules and premise.
- `总纲与分卷大纲.md`: core question, phase goals, conflict and payoff.
- `逐章大纲.md`: event, viewpoint, line advanced, payoff, hook.
- `人物档案/`: background, desire, fear, limits, current state.
- `角色语音卡/`: vocabulary, sentence rhythm, logic, address forms, emotional leakage.
- `人物关系网.md`: changing ties, debts, leverage, unresolved injuries.
- `伏笔与钩子台账.md`: plant, reminders, expected payoff, actual payoff.
- `信息差台账.md`: what reader, protagonist, allies, and opponents know.
- `时间线.md`: dated and relative events, travel and deadlines.
- `正文章节/`: versioned chapter text.

For power struggle or strategy fiction, add `势力地图.md` and one design file per major contest.

## Three-Chapter Loop

After every three chapters, pause for a small review:

- continuity of time, space, knowledge, injuries, and resources
- protagonist agency and goal movement
- character-core stability and dialogue differentiation
- hook type and long-hook repetition
- scene and image repetition
- emotional movement and delivered payoff
- AI-shaped transitions, summaries, repeated syntax, and uniform paragraphing

Fix trend errors immediately. Do not postpone a repeating prose or logic defect for dozens of chapters.

## Ten-Chapter Review

Track:

- short-term and long-term goal progress
- payoff variety and emotional distribution
- new information and world expansion
- supporting-character independent choices
- antagonist initiative and evolving pressure
- scene diversity
- unresolved hooks and foreshadowing age
- repeated plot mechanisms or chapter skeletons

Do not force a fixed number of fights, deaths, exclamation marks, or cliffhangers across all genres.

## Volume Review

- setting and number consistency
- complete timeline and travel feasibility
- character and relationship arcs
- planted/recalled/resolved hooks
- escalation and mid-volume fatigue
- promised genre experience
- ending response to the volume's core question

## Evidence Discipline

Treat platform algorithm weights, income tables, fixed retention nodes, and universal pacing numbers as time-sensitive claims. Verify against current official platform rules, editor guidance, target-category charts, and the book's own dashboard.

Do not universalize lessons from one male-oriented combat serial to Qimao female-oriented romance, period, domestic intrigue, or emotional-flow fiction. Preserve the engineering process; recalibrate the content thresholds.

## Known Rule Conflicts to Resolve Contextually

- “Every chapter must have a hook” versus intentional quiet closure.
- “One core sensory detail” versus “three senses per important scene.”
- “Every line must be functional” versus life-texture dialogue.
- “Every setup must be paid off” versus incidental detail.

Resolve with scene purpose, genre contract, causal clarity, and reader expectation. Quiet does not mean inert; texture does not mean filler; incidental detail must not masquerade as a promised clue.
