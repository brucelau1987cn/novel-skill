# Male-Channel Opening Engines (First 6 Chapters)

Three recurring entries observed on Qimao male hot charts (public early chapters, 2026-08):

## 1. Historical money-first (发家)
- Chapter 1: body-snatch / transmigrate; condemn old self; **counterattack the same chapter**.
- Chapters 2–3: cut toxic kinship; state **money-first** plan; leave the household as mid-term goal.
- Chapters 4–6: public talent market (poem/skill trade) → cash → identity leak upward.
- Transferable: economy engine before pure combat; face-saving villains; delayed true-name reveal.

## 2. Prison-return (出狱归来)
- Chapter 1: release + shameless family.
- Chapter 2: **cut kinship the same day**.
- Chapter 3: golden finger public demo (medical / true-qi) with third-party witness.
- Chapters 4–5: protect wife line + nominal marriage rules.
- Chapter 6: leave the house map (classmate / street justice) to expand.
- Transferable: cut family and protect wife on the same axis; ability must be seen by others.

## 3. Cultivation vessel-cast (铸脉升级)
- Chapter 1: finish the cheat (vessel / system / root).
- Chapters 2–3: betrayal at the gate → sect pressure → **indemnity resources**.
- Chapters 4–6: rule clarity of the cheat (absorption rate, no impurity) → collect debt from in-laws → realm breakthrough + next tournament hook.
- Transferable: power → social exchange inside chapters 1–3; do not hide the cheat for three chapters.

## Shared male rules
1. Chapters 1–3: gain power → conflict → resource/social exchange.
2. The cheat must be visible to a third party.
3. Chapter endings often: "protagonist has decided to do X".
4. Short hard chapter titles; command-style taglines.
5. Compare with female engines: decision-right payoffs still apply; male packs ability + punishment + flirtation more tightly.
