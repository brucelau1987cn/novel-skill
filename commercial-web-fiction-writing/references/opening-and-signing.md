# Opening and Signing

## Editor-Facing Opening Tiers

1. **Highly original and naturally compelling:** difficult to reproduce; potential for a time-tested phenomenon, but often polarized because there is no reliable comparison class.
2. **Strong convention plus micro-innovation:** the practical route for most commercial writers and the main training target for beginners.
3. **Promising but flawed:** the editor sees more reason to invest than reject. Repairable defects include unstable or mismatched characterization, deviation from the core attraction, causal bugs, weak emotion or conflict, and an imprecise entry point.

## Signing Criteria

Editors may assess:

- opening completion, readability, and market fit;
- author potential visible in premise, outline, creative judgment, and execution;
- stable updates, stable quality, responsiveness to useful revision, and long-term collaboration potential.

An accepted opening is only the first gate. The work still faces market data, retention, completion, and time.

## Opening Requirements

The opening should quickly establish:

- who the protagonist is;
- current predicament and urgent need;
- short-term task and long-term goal;
- central conflict;
- core selling point and promised genre experience;
- a strong hook;
- enough of the larger framework to suggest sustainability.

Enter through conflict, danger, consequential choice, or relationship change. Integrate world and relationship background into action. Keep named characters concentrated around the protagonist and a few essential roles; excessive names increase memory cost and reduce protagonist presence.

## Framework Before Drafting

Create at least a stable high-level framework. For convention-driven work, know the recurring beats, stage goals, escalation, and final resolution. A chapter-outline method can prevent opening-only strength and mid-book collapse.

## Revision Strategy

- If an editor requests a feasible revision, diagnose the target defect and revise deliberately.
- Produce alternate opening versions when the entry point remains uncertain.
- If repeated revision obscures the original promise, return to goal, motive, selling point, and target reader.
- Use market and performance data as evidence, not as a substitute for craft diagnosis.
- A weak guarantee offer or early test does not automatically invalidate the premise; determine whether the issue is packaging, opening execution, audience match, or story architecture.

## Beginner Discipline

Avoid:

- reading one book and assuming the category is mastered;
- dismissing successful ranking works without analyzing why readers choose them;
- collecting theory without repeatedly drafting and reviewing feedback;
- choosing obscure categories before building mainstream execution skill;
- abandoning every work at the first weak income or test result.

Commercial writing improves through market observation, repeated drafting, completed stories, and accumulated judgment.
